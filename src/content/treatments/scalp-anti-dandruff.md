## What it actually does

A deep-cleansing scalp treatment lifts away the buildup, excess oil, flakes and
product residue that ordinary shampoo leaves behind. Clearing that layer helps calm
an itchy, dry or dandruff-prone scalp and gives whatever you use at home a cleaner
surface to work on.

A healthy scalp is also the foundation of a regrowth plan, which is why we often start
here before anything else.

## What to expect

A thorough cleanse and treatment of the scalp, with no downtime — you can carry on
with your day straight after. Persistent or severe flaking often improves over a
short course rather than a single visit.

## When it is not the answer

Dandruff and a medical scalp condition can look alike. If what we see is psoriasis,
eczema or an infection, we will say so and point you to the right care rather than
sell you a cleanse that will not fix it.
