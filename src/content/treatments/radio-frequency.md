## What it actually does

Radio frequency delivers controlled heat into the deeper layers of the skin, which
is used to support tightening and improve the appearance of localised areas on the
face or body. It is non-invasive — no surgery, no needles, no downtime, just warm
skin for a little while after.

It works on mild laxity and gentle contouring. It is not a substitute for weight loss
or for surgery, and we will be honest at consultation about which of those your goal
really needs.

## What to expect

A comfortable, warming session, usually delivered as a short course spaced across
weeks. Results build gradually as the tissue responds, rather than appearing after a
single visit.

## Who it suits

Best for someone with a specific area and realistic expectations. We assess the area
first and tell you whether RF is the right tool — or whether Exilis, HIFU or another
approach would serve you better.
