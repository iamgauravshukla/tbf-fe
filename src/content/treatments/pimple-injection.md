## What it actually does

A pimple injection places a tiny, measured dose of anti-inflammatory corticosteroid
directly into a deep, cystic spot. It calms the inflammation at the source, so the
swelling, redness and throbbing settle quickly — often noticeably flatter within 24
to 48 hours.

It is a targeted rescue for the occasional angry breakout, not a treatment for acne
as a whole. Where breakouts are frequent or widespread, we will steer you toward a
proper acne plan rather than chasing each spot with a needle.

## What to expect

The injection itself takes only a few minutes, and most people feel little more than
a quick pinch. There is no downtime — you can head straight back to your day. Over
the next day or two the spot should soften and flatten.

## Before we treat

A doctor assesses the lesion first and uses the smallest effective dose, because too
much — or too often — can leave a temporary dent or lighten the skin. If a spot is
better left alone, or points to something that needs a different approach, we will
tell you.
