## What it actually does

Breakout-prone skin usually needs two things at once: congestion cleared and
inflammation calmed. This facial does both — a deep cleanse, professional
extractions done with clean technique rather than force, then calming care to
settle the skin instead of leaving it angry.

Squeezing at home pushes debris deeper and scars; a trained pair of hands clears
the pore and leaves the skin intact. That difference is most of what you are
paying for.

## What to expect

Extractions can sting briefly and the skin may look a little flushed for a few
hours — that is the honest trade for properly cleared pores. Deep, stubborn
breakouts take a short series of sessions, not one visit, and we will tell you
which yours is at the consultation.

## When we say no

If what you have is severe or cystic acne, a facial is not the answer and we will
not sell you one. Our doctor will look first and may recommend a pimple injection
for a single stubborn cyst, or a medical plan before any facial work begins.
