## What it actually is

An intravenous drip containing glutathione, an antioxidant that is part of the body's
own antioxidant processes. It is given by a clinician after a suitability check, and
the formulation is matched to you rather than poured from one standard bag.

We are deliberately measured about what a drip can and cannot do. It is a wellness
treatment delivered under clinical supervision, not a shortcut or a guarantee.

## Before we treat

Suitability and formulation are assessed before administration, every time. IV
treatments are not for everyone, and screening you first is not a formality — it is
the point of doing this in a clinic rather than a pop-up.

## What to expect

A comfortable session of thirty minutes to an hour with no downtime afterward. Where
a drip fits into a wider plan, we will map that out with you rather than sell single
sessions on repeat.
