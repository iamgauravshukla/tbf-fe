## What it actually does

Exosome-based products are applied **topically** to the scalp to support a healthier
environment around the follicle — the conditions in which existing hair is more likely
to hold and thicken. It is designed to complement a hair-regrowth program rather than
replace the rest of it.

We keep this treatment needle-free by design: the exosomes are applied to the surface,
never injected or microneedled. If a plan calls for stimulation, that is a separate,
clearly-explained step.

We assess the scalp and the pattern of thinning first, because the treatment supports
follicles that are still active. It cannot bring back hair where the follicle is no
longer there.

## What to expect

A course over several months, delivered as part of a plan we set with you. Any change
is gradual and is measured against where you started — we take that starting point
seriously so progress is honest, not flattering photography.

## What we will not say

We do not promise regrowth, and we are wary of anyone who does. What we will give you
is a realistic view of what is likely, and a plan we will adjust as we see how your
scalp responds.
