## What it actually does

HIFU sends focused ultrasound energy to a set depth beneath the skin — the same
support layer a surgeon works on — without breaking the surface. That controlled
heat prompts the skin to lay down new collagen, which is what firms and lifts over
the months that follow.

It remodels collagen slowly, so the result builds rather than appears. Anyone
promising an instant facelift from a machine is overselling it.

## What to expect

A single session for most faces, delivered by a clinician who sets the depth and
energy for your skin — that is a clinical decision, not a fixed setting. You will
feel heat and a deep prickling during treatment, and mild tenderness for a few days
after.

## When we say no

If your laxity is significant, surgery gives a better result and we will refer you
rather than take the booking. We review your result at twelve weeks, because that is
when the collagen change actually shows.
