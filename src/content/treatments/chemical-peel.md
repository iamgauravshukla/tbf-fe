## What it actually does

A peel uses a controlled solution to loosen and lift the damaged outer layer of
skin, so the fresher skin beneath comes through smoother and brighter. The strength
and type are chosen for your skin and your concern — a peel that suits one face can
be wrong for another.

## What to expect

A short treatment with a warm or tingling feeling, then a few days where the skin
may look flushed and flake lightly as it renews. Depth is matched to how much
downtime you can take and what your skin will tolerate, which is why we assess first
rather than run one standard peel for everyone.

## Aftercare

SPF 50 every morning, no picking at flaking skin, and no actives until we clear you.
New skin is more sun-sensitive, so protecting it is the difference between a bright
result and fresh pigmentation.
