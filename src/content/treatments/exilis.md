## What it actually does

Exilis uses controlled, energy-based heat to warm the targeted tissue, which is used
to tighten skin and improve the appearance of localised areas of concern. It is
non-invasive, so there is no surgery and no downtime — just warm skin for a short
while after.

It works on mild laxity and gentle contouring. It is not a substitute for weight loss
or for surgery, and we will tell you honestly which camp your goal sits in.

## What to expect

A comfortable, warming session, usually delivered as a short course spaced across
weeks. Results build gradually as the tissue responds, rather than appearing after a
single visit.

## Who it suits

Best for someone with a specific, localised area and realistic expectations. At
consultation we assess the area and tell you whether Exilis is the right tool — or
whether another approach, or none, would serve you better.
