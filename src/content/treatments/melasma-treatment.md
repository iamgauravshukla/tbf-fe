## What it actually does

Melasma is stubborn, hormone-driven and easily made worse by heat and aggressive
lasering. So this is a deliberately gentle, layered program — the aim is to calm the
pigment and even the tone with minimal provocation, rather than force a fast result
that rebounds darker.

The exact approach is customised to your skin, because melasma behaves differently
from person to person and from sun-damage pigmentation.

## What to expect

Steady improvement over weeks and months, not days. We will be honest that melasma is
managed rather than cured — it can return with sun, heat and hormones, and holding a
result takes maintenance and diligent SPF.

## The part that does the work

Daily SPF 50 and sun avoidance are not aftercare here — they are the treatment.
Without them, any in-clinic work is undone. We would rather set that expectation up
front than watch a good result fade.
