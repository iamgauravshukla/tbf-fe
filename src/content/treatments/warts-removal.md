## What it actually does

We remove suitable superficial growths within a specified area. The method depends on
the type, size, number and location of the lesions — there is no single technique
that fits every growth, which is why the assessment comes before the removal.

## The assessment matters most

Not everything that looks like a wart is one. A clinician checks each lesion first,
and anything suspicious or uncertain is referred for proper medical evaluation before
we touch it. We will never remove something we should have sent for a second opinion.

## What to expect

A quick, targeted treatment, then a small scab in each spot that heals over one to two
weeks depending on the method. Keep the area clean and dry, do not pick, and let us
know if anything looks inflamed rather than settling.
