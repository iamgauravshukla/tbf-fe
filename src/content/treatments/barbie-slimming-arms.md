## What it actually does

Barbie Slimming Arms is a targeted program aimed at a slimmer, more defined look
across the upper arms. The specific method is chosen for you — there is no single
protocol run on everyone — and it is built around your body and your goal.

It works with your own effort rather than replacing it, and it is a program over
several sessions rather than a one-time fix.

## What to expect

A course of sessions with little to no downtime, depending on the method we choose at
consultation. Change is gradual and is best judged over the program, not after the
first appointment.

## An honest word on goals

This is contouring and definition, not weight loss and not a medical treatment. If
your goal is better served by something else — or by working with your own routine
first — we will say so before you commit to a program.
